# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NeledPort to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sharing cheats, exploits against other people's servers, or pirated content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or other contributions that break this code, and may
block repeat offenders. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the
[NexwestLedgerCoreCompanyLimited](https://github.com/NexwestLedgerCoreCompanyLimited) GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
